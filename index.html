    05Game data in detail

    What other players can see

    Your display name, appearance (colour and cosmetics) and presence in a room are visible to other players. Leaderboards show your display name and score. Any player data you mark as public can be read by other players.

    Peer-to-peer connections

    Multiplayer sessions run over Epic Online Services. Where possible, players connect directly to each other, which means other players' devices can see your IP address. If a direct connection isn't possible, traffic goes through Epic's relay servers.

    Voice chat

    Where a game offers voice chat, it uses Meta's built-in voice chat. Your voice is sent live to other players in your session and is not recorded or stored by Aprena. Meta's handling of voice data is covered by Meta's privacy policy. You can turn the microphone permission off in your device settings.

    Moderation and bans

    Reports go to our moderation dashboard. Moderators can apply bans, which may be temporary, and can adjust in-game currency or cosmetics, for example to correct an error or remove items obtained through an exploit. We also check Epic's sanctions system for account bans.

    06Tokyn

    Status: Tokyn is in development and not yet publicly available. This section describes how it will handle data at launch. We will update this policy before any public release.

    What Tokyn processes

    Depending on the features available, this includes the prompts you write, your conversation history and files you share with it. Please don't share sensitive personal information you don't need to.

    Model training is off by default

    New accounts are opted out. Your conversations are used to train or improve our models only if you turn this on, and you can turn it off again at any time. Turning it off stops future use. It doesn't undo training that already happened.

    Retention and human review

    • Your conversation history stays in your account until you delete it.
    • If you've opted in to training, conversation data used for training is kept for up to 7 days.
    • If a conversation needs review by our team, for example for safety or quality, the reviewed data is kept for up to 1 day.
    Check that this matches how you intend it: history is kept until deleted, training copies for 7 days, human-review copies for 1 day. Also confirm where Tokyn will be hosted at launch (planned: US, Virginia). If it's outside the EU, section 08 needs to mention that transfer.

    07Meta Quest & device data

    When you play on Meta Quest, the game uses the Meta Platform SDK to:

    • check that your account owns the game (entitlement check);
    • get your Meta user ID and a one-time proof token. These are passed to Epic Online Services to log you in;
    • use your Meta username as your default display name, which you can change in-game.

    We don't request other Meta account data such as your friends list, email or location. On PC, Epic creates a device-linked login so you can play without creating an account. That credential is managed by Epic.

    Meta's handling of data on its own platform is covered by Meta's privacy policy.

    11Security

    • Connections to our backend use HTTPS encryption.
    • Multiplayer traffic is encrypted by Epic Online Services.
    • Cloud backups stored with Epic are encrypted with a 256-bit key.
    • Purchases and currency changes are checked on the server, so a modified game client can't change balances.
    • Privileged server keys are never included in player versions of our games.

    No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

    05Données de jeu en détail

    Ce que voient les autres joueurs

    Les autres joueurs voient ton pseudo, ton apparence (couleur et cosmétiques) et ta présence dans un salon. Les classements affichent ton pseudo et ton score. Les données marquées comme publiques sont lisibles par les autres joueurs.

    Connexions pair-à-pair

    Les parties multijoueurs passent par Epic Online Services. Quand c'est possible, les joueurs se connectent directement entre eux, et les appareils des autres joueurs peuvent alors voir ton adresse IP. Sinon, le trafic passe par les serveurs relais d'Epic.

    Chat vocal

    Si un jeu propose un chat vocal, il utilise ton micro uniquement avec ton autorisation. Ta voix est transmise en direct aux autres joueurs de ta session. Tu peux retirer l'autorisation dans les réglages de l'appareil.

    Modération et bannissements

    Les signalements arrivent sur notre tableau de modération. Les modérateurs peuvent appliquer des bannissements, y compris temporaires. Ils peuvent aussi ajuster la monnaie ou les cosmétiques, par exemple pour corriger une erreur ou retirer des objets obtenus via un exploit. Nous consultons aussi le système de sanctions d'Epic.

    Selon les fonctions disponibles : tes messages, ton historique de conversations et les fichiers que tu partages. Évite de partager des informations personnelles sensibles inutiles.

    Les nouveaux comptes ne participent pas. Tes conversations ne servent à entraîner ou améliorer nos modèles que si tu l'actives, et tu peux le désactiver à tout moment. La désactivation arrête l'utilisation future, mais n'annule pas l'entraînement déjà effectué.

    • Ton historique reste sur ton compte jusqu'à ce que tu le supprimes.

    Sur Meta Quest, le jeu utilise le Meta Platform SDK pour :
